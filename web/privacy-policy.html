Privacy policy

WorkAxis mobile and web app - last updated 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

WorkAxis is an attendance and workforce app operated by Yashassu Technologies, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Organizations use WorkAxis to manage their employees, branches, attendance, and returns. Each organization's administrators create the accounts for its staff.

Contact us at sureshr.apps@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and profile details: name, username, email address, phone number, employee code, designation, and branch assignments.
  • Government identifier: the Aadhaar number and Aadhaar card image entered on an employee profile.
  • Location: your device's precise location when you check in or check out, to confirm you are at your assigned branch. Location is requested only for attendance features.
  • Photos: profile photos and photos attached to returns that you or your administrators upload.
  • Files: documents uploaded as attachments to your account or to a return.
  • Attendance and return records: check-in and check-out times, breaks, locations at punch time, and the returns you submit.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and run the features your organization enables for your role.
  • To record attendance and confirm check-ins are within your assigned branch.
  • To produce attendance, return, and workforce records for your organization.
  • To keep the service secure and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

4. Who can see your information

Your organization's administrators and branch managers can see the records their role allows. Other users cannot see your information.

We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud to store and process data on our behalf, including authentication, databases, file storage, and server functions. They process data only to provide these services. We do not share your information with any other third parties.

5. Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited by role and branch, and each request is checked on the server. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider and are not readable by administrators.

Our servers, databases, and file storage are hosted in India on Google Cloud. No system is completely secure, so please tell us promptly if you think your account has been compromised.

6. How long we keep information

Account and profile details are kept while the account exists. Attendance and return records, and the photos and documents attached to them, are kept for 90 days and then deleted.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and personal data at our deletion request page, or by emailing sureshr.apps@gmail.com. Your organization's administrator can also remove your account. Where attendance or return records must be kept, the account is suspended and your personal details are removed as far as those records allow.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate information. Your administrator can also update most profile details.
  • You can ask us to delete your data, as described above.
  • You can withdraw location access at any time in your device settings. Check-in and check-out will then stop working.

9. Children

WorkAxis is for employees and is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
